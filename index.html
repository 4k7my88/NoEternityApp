no Eternity

A generative, iterative and interactive screen-saver artwork. By your touch, it changes.

I combine analogue feeling and digital technologies in the creation of my work. I created composite collages that reflect on relationships between the eternity and its image. There are no eternity.

How to play

  • Move or drag to paint bands of colour. Every finger paints its own band.
  • Hold the dissolve button to dissolve what is there.
  • Leave it alone and it goes on painting itself, like a screen saver.
  • Save keeps this moment as a picture. It will not come back.

Keys: arrows move · Space dissolve · S save · F full screen · I about

First made in 2015 with Processing, as an Android app. See the original sketch.
